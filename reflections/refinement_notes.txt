Why refinement matters before coding:
Vague steps force a teammate or my future self to guess what to do, and two readers could produce two different programs from the same draft. When the rules are written down, I can trace the logic by hand and catch mistakes before any syntax is involved. Refinement turns an intention into an instruction that produces the same result every time.
